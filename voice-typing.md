# Voice typing

## What it is and why it exists

Voice typing turns what you say into written words in a text box. You speak, and the words appear where you would have typed them. It is for when typing is slow, awkward on a phone, or when you simply think better out loud. You can still edit the text before you send it.

This is different from a [voice call]({{ '/voice-calls/' | relative_url }}). A call is a live two-way talk with one Bot. Voice typing only fills in a text box, and you decide when to send.

## Before you start

- **A microphone** and a browser that lets this site use it.
- **A browser that supports it.** If yours does not, Settings says "Voice typing isn't supported in this browser." and the mic button is hidden.
- **Cost:** none from VectoBots. The Settings card marks voice typing language as **Free**.
- **Privacy:** your browser turns speech into text. **In Chrome, the sound is sent to Google to be turned into text.** That is how Chrome does it, and the Voice page notes it too.

## How to use it

1. Open a chat, or another box that has a small **microphone** button.
2. Tap the microphone. It lights up while it listens. If the browser asks to use your microphone, allow it.
3. Speak. Words appear as you talk, in a dimmer style while they might still change.
4. Tap the microphone again to stop. The button's label then returns to "Voice typing".
5. Read the text, fix anything, and send it as usual.

Details that help:

- Words are added at the end of what is already in the chat box. In other boxes, they go where your cursor is.
- Only one microphone listens at a time. Starting another stops the first.
- Sending your message stops listening and drops anything still pending.

**Where the microphone appears.** In the chat box, and in many other text boxes in the app, such as a group chat's goal, a Bot's personality or "What it should know" boxes, the note on a rule, and comment boxes. The boxes that have it show a small microphone at the bottom right.

## Setting the language

- **Laptop:** open **Settings**, pick **Notifications** on the left, and find the card **Voice typing**.
- **Phone:** tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then **Notifications**, and scroll to **Voice typing**. Use the back arrow to return.

1. In the row **Language**, open the menu.
2. Choose **English** or **Español**.
3. You will see "Saved."

If you never choose, the app picks for you: Spanish (Mexico) if the app is in Spanish or your browser is set to Spanish, otherwise English (United States).

The choice is stored in this browser only, so on another device you set it again. Speak in the language you chose. A mismatch gives poor text.

On the Voice page, a card called **Talking in groups** repeats the privacy note and has a **Learn more** link to this guide.

## Talking to a group out loud

In a group chat, a Huddle or a Sprint there is also a **Talk** button beside the microphone. It opens a "Talk to the chat" screen where your spoken words are sent to the chat like typed ones, and the Bots' answers are read aloud one sentence at a time, each Bot in its own voice. You can cut in.

- **Mute** turns your microphone off, and **Skip** skips the answer being read.
- It needs **Chrome or Edge**. Elsewhere you will see: "Talking to a group needs Chrome or Edge. Use the call button in a one-Bot chat instead."
- In a Sprint, your words go to the Sprint's lead Bot.
- If typing is off in the chat, talking is off too.
- Talk ends if you leave the chat.

This uses the same speech-to-text as voice typing, so the same privacy note applies.

## Real examples

- **A coach** on a phone dictates the notes of a session into a Bot's chat while walking to the train, then fixes two words and sends.
- **A consultant** speaks a long brief for a Huddle, then reads it on screen before sending, because spoken text sometimes needs commas.
- **A therapist** dictates a general to-do list ("remind me to renew my liability insurance"), and keeps client details out of dictated text. In Chrome the sound goes to Google.

## Costs and limits

- No cost from VectoBots, shown as **Free**.
- Accuracy depends on your microphone, your voice and the language you chose.

## Common problems and fixes

- **"Microphone access is blocked. Allow it in your browser settings."** Allow the microphone for this site in your browser, then tap the microphone again.
- **"Voice typing stopped. Try again."** The listening cut out. Tap the microphone again.
- **"Voice typing isn't available in this browser."** Try Chrome or Edge.
- **The words come out in the wrong language.** Open Settings, Notifications, Voice typing, Language, and choose the one you speak.
- **No microphone button.** The browser may not support voice typing.
