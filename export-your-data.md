# Export your data

## What it is and why it exists

Your VectoBots belongs to you. **Export your data** lets you download a copy of your work in one file, any time, so you can keep a backup, move to another place or simply know it is safe. You do not need to ask anyone or open a support request.

![Settings on a laptop, page You: the Your data box with Download everything, Memory as text and Memory for backup, and the Bot templates box below it]({{ '/images/export-laptop.png' | relative_url }})

*The "Your data" section on the You page.*

## Before you start

- It is free and always available to you, the owner. Bots cannot use it.
- Making the file takes a moment. Larger accounts take longer. Keep the page open until your browser starts the download.
- The file goes to your normal Downloads folder, so make sure you have room there.

## How to download everything

**On a laptop**

1. Click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**.
2. Click **You** in the list on the left.
3. Scroll to **Your data**. The first box is **Download everything**.
4. Click **Download everything**. Your browser saves one file named like `vectobots-everything-2026-11-23.zip`, with the day's date.

**On a phone**

1. Tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then tap **You**.
2. Scroll to **Your data** and tap **Download everything**.
3. Your phone saves the file in its downloads. Use the back arrow to return to the list.

A ZIP file is a folder packed into one file. On most laptops and phones you open it with a tap or a double click.

## What the download holds

- **Your memory**, as readable text and as a backup file.
- **Every chat** with your Bots.
- **Your Bots as template files** (their name, face and instructions, no secrets).
- **The latest version of every file and board** in your Library and Boards.
- A short **README** inside the ZIP that explains the contents. It also lists any file left out for being too big.

## What is never included

- **Keys and sign-ins.** Passwords, AI keys and connected accounts are never in the file. After a move you sign in and connect them again.
- **Your Vault.** It has its own export, on purpose, because it holds your saved passwords.

If you have a lot of large videos or audio, a single very big file (over about 1 GB) is left out of the ZIP and the README says so. The ZIP also stops growing before it reaches about 3.5 GB.

## The two smaller buttons

Next to **Download everything** there are two more buttons for your memory only:

- **Memory as text.** A plain, readable file you can open and read, for example to review what your Bots remember about you.
- **Memory for backup.** A backup copy of your memory, meant for safekeeping. It is less comfortable to read.

Memory stays on your server. These downloads are copies. Nothing is deleted when you download.

## Exporting a single file or board

Separate from the big download, you can take a copy of one thing a Bot made:

1. Open the file or board in your Library or Boards.
2. Use its **Download** menu. It says **Download as** and lists the formats that work on your server, always including **The file as made**.
3. Pick one. A message says "Making the file…" and your browser saves it.

If a format needs a tool your server does not have yet, the menu says "N more formats need a tool this server does not have yet." The original file is always there.

## Examples for a solo practice

- **A monthly backup.** On the first of the month, press **Download everything** and move the ZIP to an encrypted drive you control.
- **Before a big change.** Before you change your plan or your hosting, download everything first.
- **A client file.** Download one report as a PDF from its **Download** menu to send to a colleague yourself. Do not send a file that contains client names unless the client agreed.
- **Confidentiality.** The ZIP holds your chats and memory, which may contain client information. Treat it like any client record. Store it encrypted, and do not leave it on a shared computer or in an open cloud folder.

## Costs and limits

- Exporting is free.
- A single file over about 1 GB is left out of the ZIP, and the ZIP stops at about 3.5 GB in total. The README lists what was left out.

## Common problems and fixes

- **The download fails.** Try again in a minute. If it keeps failing, the server may be busy or full.
- **"Couldn't make that file. The original is still there."** That format did not work. Choose **The file as made**.
- **"This page loads parts from the internet, so the copy may look different."** A converted copy of a web page may not match the original exactly.
- **Nothing downloads.** Allow downloads for this site in your browser settings and try again.
- **I cannot find the file.** Look in your browser's Downloads list.
- **My passwords are not in it.** That is on purpose. Use the Vault export.
