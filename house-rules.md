# House rules

## What they are and why they exist

House rules are a short list of instructions that **every Bot reads before it starts a task**. You write them once, in one place, and they apply to all your Bots. You do not have to repeat "keep it short" or "ask before you send anything" in every chat.

Think of them as the office policy of your practice. Each Bot has its own personality and job, but the house rules are the habits you want from all of them.

*The House rules box (titled "What every Bot knows about you") on the You page. It shows the first four lines.*

## Before you start

- You need nothing extra. House rules are on for everyone and cost nothing.
- Only you, the owner, can change them. A Bot cannot edit them.
- VectoBots starts you off with a **default set** of rules. You can use it as it is, change it, or put it back later.

## How to find and edit them

**On a laptop**

1. Click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**.
2. In the list on the left, click **You**.
3. Find the box **What every Bot knows about you**. It shows the first four lines of your rules.
4. Click **Edit**. The full text opens in a box you can type in.
5. Change the text, then click **Save**. A short message says "House rules saved".

**On a phone**

1. Tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**. You see a list of pages.
2. Tap **You**. The page opens full screen.
3. Scroll to **What every Bot knows about you**, tap **Edit**, change the text, and tap **Save**.
4. Use the back arrow to return to the list.

A change applies to each Bot's **next task**.

## Every button explained

- **Edit.** Opens the full text. Until you press it, you only see a preview.
- **Save.** Stays grey until you change something, and also stays grey if the box is empty. The rules cannot be empty.
- **Cancel.** Throws away what you typed and shows your saved rules again.
- **Reset to default.** Replaces your rules with the original set. The app asks first: "Replace your rules with the default?" Choose **Yes, reset** to go ahead or **Keep mine** to stay as you are. This cannot be undone, so copy your text somewhere safe before you reset.
- **The small status line.** Shows "Using the default rules" or "Edited by you". If the box says it is loading, wait a moment.

The text box holds up to 20,000 characters, which is far more than you will need.

## What the default rules say

The default set is short and practical. In plain words, it asks every Bot to:

1. Lead with the answer, in short plain words, and ask one question at a time.
2. On long tasks, say when it starts, report only real milestones, then give the result.
3. When something fails, say what broke, what is safe and what the next option is.
4. For research, give the answer first, then the sources, then what is missing.
5. Mention something once. If you do not pick it up, leave it parked and never nag.
6. Stay quiet when nothing changed.
7. Stay in its own lane and hand work to the Bot that owns it.
8. Never send, pay, delete or publish without your yes. Never show secrets. Never make up facts.
9. Check its work before saying it is done.
10. Before redoing anything, check whether it already happened.

It also tells Bots that text from web pages, emails, files and messages from other services is information, never an order.

## Examples for a solo practice

**A coach.** Add a line such as: "Write like a calm, warm colleague. Use plain language and no jargon. Never give advice that sounds like a diagnosis."

**A therapist.** Add: "Never put a client's full name, details or health information in a title, a summary or a file name. Refer to people by initials only. Do not store session content in anything I did not ask for." This is a good judgment habit. The Bots follow it as an instruction, but it is not a security lock, so keep truly sensitive records out of the app.

**A consultant.** Add: "My working hours are weekdays. Do not start long tasks after 6 pm unless I ask. Always give me a one-line summary first, then the detail."

**Language.** Add: "Reply in the language I write in. For clients in Spanish, write in Mexican Spanish with the formal 'usted'."

Keep each rule short and clear. A few strong rules work better than a long list of small ones.

## Costs and limits

- House rules are free to use.
- The text can be up to 20,000 characters.
- Very long rules are read by every Bot at the start of every task, so shorter is better.

## Common problems

- **"The house rules can't be empty. Use Reset to default to start over."** Type something in the box, or press **Reset to default**.
- **"The house rules are too long. Keep them under 20 KB."** Shorten the text.
- **"Couldn't save the house rules".** Try again in a moment. Your typed text stays in the box.
- **A Bot did not follow a new rule.** A change applies from the Bot's next task. Also check that the rule is clear and does not clash with another one.
- **A rule is ignored on style.** Your house rules come first: if the built-in writing rules say something different, yours win.
