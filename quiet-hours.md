# Quiet hours

Quiet hours keep routine messages from reaching you at night.

- Pick the time quiet hours start and end.
- **Routine messages** are held while it is quiet. They arrive together with your next briefing.
- **Urgent routines** still come through.

The clock follows your time zone in About you.
