# Quiet hours

Quiet hours are a window of time, usually your night, when your Bots do not interrupt you with routine things. Messages that run on a schedule are held back, and Bots do not send you their own check-ins. It exists so a Bot that works around the clock does not light up your phone while you sleep, or while you are in a session with a client.

## Before you start

- Nothing to buy. It is free and part of every plan.
- Check your **Time zone** too. See [About you]({{ '/about-you/' | relative_url }}).
- Decide two times: when you want quiet to begin and when you want it to end.

The app starts with quiet from 10:00 PM until 7:30 AM.

## How to use it, step by step

**On a laptop:** click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**. **You** is already selected. Look for the card called **Quiet hours**.

**On a phone:** tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then **You**, then find the **Quiet hours** card in the **General** section.

1. Click the **Quiet from** box and choose the time quiet begins, for example 10:00 PM.
2. Click the **Quiet until** box and choose the time it ends, for example 7:30 AM.
3. That is all. There is no Save button. The app saves each change and shows "Saved."

## Every option explained

**Quiet from.** The time the quiet window starts. That minute is already quiet.

**Quiet until.** The time it ends. That minute is no longer quiet.

**The window can cross midnight.** From 10:00 PM until 7:30 AM works as you would expect: it covers the night. You can also pick a daytime window, for example 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM for a long lunch.

**Same time in both boxes.** If you choose the same time for start and end, there are no quiet hours at all. This is the way to turn them off.

**Both boxes must be filled.** If one is empty you see "Pick a start and an end time for quiet hours." and the app puts back what was there.

## What is held, and what still comes through

- **Routines.** A routine is a task a Bot does on a schedule. If one runs inside your quiet window, its reply is held: no unread mark, no ping, no Slack message. In the routine's history, that run is tagged **Held for the morning**.
- **Urgent routines.** When you create or edit a routine, there is a switch called **Urgent**, with the line "Tell me even during quiet hours". Turn it on for something that must reach you at any hour. It is off by default.
- **Run now.** If you press **Run now** on a routine yourself, it is never held.
- **Check-ins.** Some Bots may send you a short check-in on their own. That depends on how much you let them do on their own. During quiet hours they are told not to send one. The app says so: it is never sent during quiet hours.

Quiet hours do not stop you. You can write to any Bot at any hour.

## Real examples

- **A therapist** sets quiet from 9:00 PM to 8:00 AM so no Bot reaches out during family time or before the first session. A routine that checks tomorrow's schedule is marked **Urgent** only if it must come at night, and usually it does not.
- **A coach who runs evening groups** moves quiet to start at midnight, because evenings are working hours.
- **A consultant who flies often** changes **Time zone** when they land, so the times in the app match where they are.
- Do not use an urgent routine for anything about a client's safety. A Bot is not a crisis service. Keep your own emergency plan.

A Bot can also ask you to change quiet hours. You will see a card such as "Anchor wants quiet hours to start at 9:00 PM instead of 10:00 PM." You approve or deny it. Nothing changes without your answer, and after approving you can undo it.

## Costs and limits

- Free. No extra AI use.
- One window per day, the same every day. There are no separate weekend times.
- Bot check-ins follow the clock of your **Time zone**.

## Common problems and fixes

- **"Pick a start and an end time for quiet hours."** One of the boxes was empty. Fill both.
- **A message came during the night.** It may be an **Urgent** routine, a run you started yourself with **Run now**, or a message in a chat. Quiet hours only hold routine runs and Bot check-ins.
- **The quiet window starts at the wrong hour.** Check the two times, and check **Time zone** in the card above.
- **I want no quiet hours.** Put the same time in both boxes.
