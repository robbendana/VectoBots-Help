# Temp files

Your Bots work on a computer, and while they work they leave scratch files and downloads behind. Over weeks this can fill the disk. **Temp files** is a one-button clean-up that removes the old ones so you never run out of room. It always shows you what it will delete first, and it deletes nothing until you say so.

## Before you start

- Nothing to buy. The clean-up is free and uses no AI.
- Anything you cleaned is gone for good. There is no Trash for this.
- It only touches files that are more than 7 days old.

## Where to find it

**On a laptop:** click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**, then **Computer**. At the top you see the disk meters, and the **Temp files** row below them.

**On a phone:** tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then **Computer**. The same rows appear. Use the back arrow to return.

## How to use it, step by step

1. Open **Settings**, then **Computer**.
2. Look at the meters. Each shows free space and how much its own folder uses.
3. In the **Temp files** row, press **Clean up**. The app says "Checking..." and counts what is old enough.
4. You then see one of two things.
   - "Nothing to clean up. No files older than 7 days." Nothing to do.
   - A question: "Delete 12 old files and free about 340 MB? This can't be undone." The numbers are yours.
5. If you agree, press **Delete files**. If not, press **Cancel**. Cancel changes nothing.
6. After deleting, you see "Removed 12 files, freed 340 MB." with a list of what was removed, and a short "Freed 340 MB" message.

If the list is long, the app shows the first part and says "And N more."

## Every option explained

**The disk meters.** There are two: **App data** and **Workspace**. Each shows "X free of Y · this folder uses Z" and a bar that fills as space is used. Next to it you may see **Getting full** (above about 85 percent used) or **Low space**. When free space drops under 10 percent, a red notice appears at the top of the page: "Disk space is low on ... : under 10% free. Clean up temp files below." If the app cannot read the size, it says "Free space unknown."

**Temp files.** Deletes files more than 7 days old. It looks in the workspace's temporary folder and in every folder called Downloads.

**Clean up.** The button that runs the check. It only counts. The deleting is a second, separate step.

**Delete files.** The red button that really deletes. This is the point of no return.

**Skipped links.** If the app finds a shortcut that points outside the workspace, it does not follow it, and says "Skipped N links that point outside the workspace." This protects the rest of your computer.

## What is not touched

- Files newer than 7 days.
- Your Bots' own documents and work, outside temp and Downloads folders.
- Your memory, chats and settings.

When a Bot saved something important only in a Downloads folder, move it before you clean up. After 7 days it is fair game.

## Real examples

- **A coach who makes workshop videos with a Bot's help** sees the disk marked **Getting full**. They download what they want to keep, then press **Clean up**, and free a few gigabytes.
- **A therapist** keeps no client material in downloads on purpose. A monthly **Clean up** keeps the server tidy. Remember that confidentiality means removing scratch copies too, and this does it for files older than 7 days.
- **A consultant** checks the meters once a month. If it says "Nothing to clean up," they are done in ten seconds.

## Costs and limits

- Free.
- Age: more than 7 days. This is fixed. You cannot change the number.
- No undo. Deleted means gone.
- The page's meters show two places: **App data** and **Workspace**.

## Common problems and fixes

- **"Nothing to clean up. No files older than 7 days."** Everything is recent. Try again later.
- **"Disk space is low ... Clean up temp files below."** Run **Clean up**. If that frees little, the space is held by your own files, so review what Bots saved.
- **"Skipped N links that point outside the workspace."** Normal and safe. Nothing outside the workspace was touched.
- **"Something went wrong. Try again."** Wait a moment and press **Clean up** again.
- **The meters say "Free space unknown."** The app could not read the disk. Ask whoever runs your server.
