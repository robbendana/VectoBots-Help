# Temp files

Cleans up space on your server.

- It removes files **older than 7 days** from the temp folder and from any Downloads folders in the workspace.
- You see how many files and how much space first. **Nothing is deleted until you confirm**, and deleting cannot be undone.
- Doing it costs nothing.
