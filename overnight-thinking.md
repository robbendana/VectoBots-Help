# Overnight thinking

Once a night your Bots' notes are thought over, and you get a short note in **Today**: connections, patterns, open questions and ideas for next steps.

- It is **one switch for every Bot**, not for the Bot whose tab is open.
- Ideas are **not saved as memories** unless you keep them.
- The notes your Bots keep, with what they wrote about people, are sent to the AI company named on the screen for this. The screen also shows the model, a **daily spending limit** and what you spent in the last 30 days.
- You can switch it off at any time. You can also run it once yourself with *Think now*.
