# Overnight thinking

Overnight thinking is an optional habit for your Bots. Once a night the notes your Bots keep are thought over, and a short note appears in **Today**. It holds connections between things, patterns, open questions and ideas for next steps.

It exists to give you the kind of thinking you rarely have time for: someone reading across everything and saying "these two things belong together" or "you said one thing last week and another this week."

## Before you start

- **It is off until you turn it on.**
- It needs an AI connection of its own. The screen names the company and the model that do the thinking. If that connection is missing, the screen says so, and **Think now** is greyed out.
- It costs a small amount, with a daily limit. See "Costs and limits".
- **Read the privacy section below first.** This feature sends your Bots' notes to an outside AI company.

## Privacy: what leaves your server

The notes your Bots keep, **with what they wrote about people**, are sent to the AI company named on the screen for this. That is not the same as the AI each Bot uses for chat.

If your Bots keep notes about clients, think before you switch this on. Coaches, therapists and consultants have a duty of confidentiality. Do not turn it on if your notes could identify a client and you have not decided you are comfortable with that. You can keep it off and lose nothing else in the app.

## How to turn it on

The switch lives on the **Memory** page, in a row at the top called **Overnight thinking (all Bots)**. The row shows "Up to $0.25 a night" by default, which is the daily limit.

1. Open **More**, then **Memory**. This works the same on a laptop and on a phone.
2. Find the row **Overnight thinking (all Bots)** and open it if it is closed.
3. Read the line about where your notes are sent.
4. Tap **Turn on**. The app says "Overnight thinking is on. It runs after 3 AM."

To stop, tap **Turn off**. The app says "Overnight thinking is off."

## Every button and line explained

- **One switch for every Bot.** It is not per Bot, and it is not for only the Bot whose tab is open.
- **Turn on / Turn off.** Starts or stops the nightly habit. The run happens after 3 AM in your own time zone.
- **Think now.** Runs it once, right now, without waiting for night. The app says "Thinking. This takes a minute or two." The button is greyed while it runs or when no AI connection is set up.
- **Read the last one.** Opens the most recent note.
- **Undo the last one.** Appears when the last run can be undone.
- **The status line.** Shows what happened last: the time, a short comment and what that run cost; "Last night was skipped" with the reason; "Last try did not finish"; or "Nothing yet."
- **The sentence about spending.** Names the AI company and the model, states the daily limit, and shows what you spent in the **last 30 days**.

## What the morning note contains

A short note in **Today** with connections, patterns, open questions and ideas for next steps. It points back to the notes it is based on.

Ideas are **not saved as memories** unless you keep them. Nothing in your Bots' memory changes just because the note exists.

## Costs and limits

- There is a **daily spending limit**, shown on the screen ("Up to $0.25 a night" until it is changed). The nightly run and **Think now** **share this one limit** for the same day. If **Think now** has already used it, the nightly run can be skipped.
- The app works out before the run whether it can stay within the limit. If not, it skips quietly instead of overspending.
- If the chosen model has no known price, the run is skipped with the message "This model has no known price, so the nightly cap cannot be kept. Pick another model."
- Its cost shows in [Costs]({{ '/costs/' | relative_url }}) and counts toward the month's spending, which is what your [Monthly limit]({{ '/monthly-limit/' | relative_url }}) measures. The per-night limit is a separate, smaller cap.

## Example

A consultant works with several clients and keeps notes in her Bots. She decides client names must not leave her server, so she keeps overnight thinking off. Another user, a coach who keeps only general notes about her own business plans, turns it on. One morning her note says that three different notes mention raising her prices, and asks whether she wants to decide before the autumn. She keeps that idea.

## Common problems

- **Think now is greyed out.** The AI connection for this feature is not set up on the server.
- **"Last night was skipped."** Read the reason after it, such as no connection or the day's limit already reached.
- **"Last try did not finish."** Tap **Think now** to try again.
- **I did not get a note.** The switch is off, the run was skipped, or it has not run yet (it starts after 3 AM).
