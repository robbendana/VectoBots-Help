# Let Bots use your laptop

A helper app on your laptop lets a Bot use your screen when you allow it.

1. Install the helper on your laptop.
2. Make a code here and enter it in the helper once.
3. Switch on the Bots that may ask.

The **first action of each session asks you on a card**. Choose to allow it for this task or for 30 minutes. To stop asking for a Bot, add a rule under *What Bots may do*.

The Vault, password managers, banking pages and sign-in screens are **always blocked**. Every action is listed in Activity.
