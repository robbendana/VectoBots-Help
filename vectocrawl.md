# VectoCrawl

VectoCrawl lets your Bots look things up online so their answers are current. It works with no account.

- It covers searching, reading pages and short research runs. Social networks are searched through what search engines have listed.
- **Free search** is used first if you have your own search server, and last otherwise. It can be slow or refused when busy.
- **Your own search server** is optional: the address of a SearXNG server you run.
- Add a key only if you want better results. See [Better results with keys]({{ '/vectocrawl-keys/' | relative_url }}).
