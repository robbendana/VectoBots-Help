# About you

Your photo, your name and a short note about who you are.

- **Photo.** Shows next to what you say in chats. Pick a JPG, PNG or WebP picture. It is cropped to a square and kept on your server.
- **Name.** What your Bots call you.
- **About me.** A line or two, for example your job or where you live. Kept for your Bots to use later. You can edit or empty it at any time.
- **Time zone.** Sets the clock for briefings and quiet hours.

Every change saves by itself.
