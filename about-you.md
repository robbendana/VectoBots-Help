# About you

"About you" is the small card where you tell VectoBots who you are: your photo, your name, a short note about yourself and your time zone. It exists so the app feels like yours. Your face and name appear in your chats, and your time zone makes every clock in the app match the one on your wall.

It takes about a minute to fill in, and you can change any of it later.

## Before you start

- You need nothing special. It is free and part of every plan.
- A picture on your device if you want a photo. JPG, PNG and WebP pictures work.
- Nothing is sent to anyone outside your own setup. The photo and the text stay on your server.

## Where to find it

**On a laptop:** click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**. The list on the left starts on **You**, and the **General** section is right there on the page. The card at the top is called **About you**.

**On a phone:** tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then **You**, then look for **General**. Use the back arrow at the top to return to the list.

## How to use it, step by step

1. Open **Settings**, then **You**, then **General**.
2. Next to **Your photo**, tap **Upload photo** and pick a picture from your device.
3. Click in the **Your name** box and type the name you want your Bots to use for you.
4. Click in the **About me** box and write a line or two about who you are.
5. Open **Time zone** and pick the city closest to where you live.

You never press a Save button. Each change saves by itself as soon as you leave the box or pick from the list. A small "Saved." message confirms it.

## Every option explained

**Your photo.** The picture shows next to what you say in your chats. The app shrinks it and crops it to a centred square, then shows it round. You see the new picture at once, then you see "Photo saved." If you ever want it gone, tap **Remove**. You will see "Photo removed." and the app goes back to the plain circle with your first letter. The **Remove** button only appears while you have a photo.

If a picture will not load you may see "Pick a JPG, PNG or WebP picture." or "Could not read that picture." Try a different file.

**Your name.** This is what your Bots call you. It is required, up to 60 characters. If you empty the box the app says "Enter your name." and puts your old name back.

**About me.** A line or two on who you are. Up to 500 characters. The box suggests an example: "coach and psychologist in Mexico City." You can edit it or empty it at any time. It is optional.

**Time zone.** A list of cities from every part of the world, plus UTC. The default is whatever your setup was first made with, and for many people that is Mexico City. The time zone sets the clock for the times you see in the app. It is also the clock that **Quiet hours** and the daily check-in follow. See [Quiet hours]({{ '/quiet-hours/' | relative_url }}).

On a phone, the list of Settings pages shows a summary line for **General**, such as "Ana · Mexico City time · quiet 10 PM to 7:30 AM". It is a quick way to check these settings without opening them.

## Real examples

- **A therapist with a private practice** writes "Therapist in private practice, sessions Monday to Thursday." That is enough for the Bots to know your working rhythm. Leave client details out. Do not write names, diagnoses or anything a client told you. This box is for you, not for your clients.
- **A coach who travels** keeps the time zone on the city where they live, and changes it when they move for a few weeks. Briefings and quiet hours then follow them.
- **A consultant who shares a computer** adds a photo so they can tell at a glance that a chat is theirs.

A good rule for this card: describe yourself and how you work. Never put in anything you would not want read out loud.

## Costs and limits

- Free. Nothing here uses your AI plan or adds to your monthly spend.
- Name: required, 60 characters at most.
- About me: 500 characters at most.
- Photo: JPG, PNG or WebP. The app shrinks it before saving, so a large picture is fine.
- The time zone list is fixed to the cities in the list. You cannot type your own.

## Common problems and fixes

- **"Enter your name."** The name box was empty. Type a name and click away.
- **"Pick a JPG, PNG or WebP picture."** The file is another kind, such as a PDF or an HEIC photo. Convert it or pick another.
- **"Could not save the picture."** The photo did not reach your server. Check your connection and try again.
- **The times in the app look wrong.** Check **Time zone**. It is probably set to another city.
- **Nothing happens when I change a box.** Click or tap outside the box so the change registers. Look for "Saved."
