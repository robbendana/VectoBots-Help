# Archive

## What it is and why it exists

Over time you collect group chats, Huddles and Sprints you no longer use every day. **Archive** tucks them away so your side list stays tidy, without losing anything. Everything inside is kept. You can bring it back with one tap, or delete it for good when you are sure.

The rule is simple: **archive is safe, delete is final.**

![Settings on a laptop, page Archived: it says Nothing archived, and that archived Group chats, Huddles and Sprints go here]({{ '/images/archive-laptop.png' | relative_url }})

*The Archived page, with nothing in it yet.*

## Before you start

- It is free and on for everyone.
- Archive works on **Group chats**, **Huddles** and **Sprints**. A chat with a single Bot is not archived. You can clear it instead (see below).
- Costs, Activity and approvals are records. They are never deleted by archiving or deleting.

## How to archive

**A Group chat or a Huddle**

1. Open the group chat or Huddle.
2. Open its options menu and choose **Archive**.
3. A message says "Archived" and the name, with an **Undo** button. Tap **Undo** right away if it was a mistake.
4. On a laptop you land back on Today. On a phone you return to the list.

**A Sprint**

A Sprint is archived by **closing** it. Use **Close sprint** when its work is done. A closed Sprint then appears on the Archived page.

## How to look at what you archived

**On a laptop**

1. Click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**.
2. Click **Archived** in the list on the left.

**On a phone**

1. Tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**.
2. Tap **Archived**. Use the back arrow to return to the list.

Each line shows the name, what it is (Group chat, Huddle or Sprint) and when it last changed. The newest are first. If there is nothing, the page says "Nothing archived".

## Every button explained

- **Bring back.** For a Group chat or Huddle. It returns to your list and works as before. A message says "{Name} is back."
- **Open.** For a Sprint. It opens the closed Sprint so you can read what happened and see what it made.
- **Delete.** Removes the item **for good**. The app asks first, see below.

## What Delete does

When you choose **Delete**, a window asks "Delete {name}?" and explains what stays. Then you click **Delete** to confirm, or cancel.

- **Group chat or Huddle:** "This removes the group chat for good. The Bots keep what they saved in memory. Costs stay in Costs."
- **Sprint:** "This removes the sprint for good. What it made stays in the Library and Boards. Costs stay in Costs."

After you confirm there is no undo. A message says "Deleted {name}."

## Clearing a chat is different

A chat with one Bot has a **Clear chat** choice in its options. It asks "Clear this chat?" and tells you the messages are removed, the Bot starts a fresh conversation and **keeps its memory**. Costs stay in Costs. Use it when you want a clean slate without losing what the Bot knows.

## Examples for a solo practice

- **End of a project.** You ran a Sprint to prepare a workshop. When it is finished, close it. It moves to Archived, and the files it made stay in your Library.
- **A seasonal group chat.** Your "Summer retreat" group chat is quiet until next year. Archive it. In spring, bring it back.
- **Confidentiality.** If a group chat contains client details you no longer need, archiving is not enough, because archived things **keep everything**. Delete it instead, and remember that what the Bots saved in memory stays. Review that in your Bots' memory as well.
- **Tidy list, no risk.** When in doubt, archive first. You can delete later, once you are sure.

## Costs and limits

- Archiving and deleting are free.
- Costs are never erased. Your spending history stays in Costs.

## Common problems and fixes

- **I archived something by mistake.** Tap **Undo** on the message, or open **Archived** and tap **Bring back**.
- **I cannot find a Sprint I closed.** It is on the Archived page. Closed Sprints use **Open**, not **Bring back**.
- **"Couldn't do that."** The action did not go through. Try again.
- **"Couldn't load that."** The Archived page did not load. Reopen it.
- **I deleted something and want it back.** Delete is final. Use your export to look for a copy, see [Export your data]({{ '/export-your-data/' | relative_url }}).
