# Install the app

VectoBots runs in your browser, but you can put it on your home screen or desktop so it opens in its own window, full screen, like any other app. It exists so you can reach your Bots with one tap, without hunting for a browser tab.

Installing does not move anything. Your chats, Bots and files stay on your server. The installed app is just a faster way to open the same thing.

## Before you start

- Nothing to buy. Free, and part of every plan.
- You need to be signed in to VectoBots in your browser first.
- Use a browser that can install apps: Chrome or Edge on a laptop or Android phone, Safari on an iPhone or iPad.

## Where to find it

**On a laptop:** click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**, choose **You**, and scroll to the **This device** section. The first row is called **App**.

**On a phone:** tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then **You**, then find **This device**. Use the back arrow to return to the list.

## How to use it, step by step

What you see in the **App** row depends on your browser. If the row has no button, use the steps below for your browser.

**Chrome or Edge, with an Install button:**
1. In **This device**, find the **App** row.
2. Press **Install app**.
3. Your browser asks you to confirm. Accept.
4. The row changes to a green **Installed** tag.

**Chrome or Edge, without an Install button:**
1. Open the browser menu (the three dots), and choose **Install app**. Some versions call it "Install VectoBots".
2. Confirm.

**Safari on an iPhone or iPad:**
1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

Open the app from its new icon. It opens without the browser's address bar.

## Every option explained

**App.** Shows the state of the install and the one step you need.
- **Install app** button: your browser is ready, so one press starts the install.
- **Installed** tag: you are already using it as an app. No button is needed.
- No button and no tag: your browser cannot install with one press. Use your browser's own menu, as in the steps above.

**Signed in.** You stay signed in on this device until you sign out. Press **Sign out** to leave this device. You will need to sign in again afterwards. This is the right button for a shared or borrowed device.

## Real examples

- **A coach on the way to a session** puts VectoBots on the phone's home screen and opens a Bot in one tap to dictate a quick note.
- **A therapist at the desk** installs it on the office laptop, so it sits in its own window beside the calendar and does not get lost among browser tabs.
- **A consultant on a shared computer** does not install, or presses **Sign out** when done, so the next person cannot open the chats. Never leave client work signed in on a device that is not yours.

## Costs and limits

- Free. Installing adds no cost and uses no AI.
- You install once per device. A laptop and a phone are two installs.
- The app needs your server to work. If the server is off, the installed app cannot reach your Bots either.

## Common problems and fixes

- **There is no Install app button.** Your browser may not offer one, or the app is already installed. Use the steps above for your browser. If you only see **Add shortcut** in the browser menu, reload the page once and look again.
- **Safari has no Install app.** Safari uses Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **It still opens inside the browser.** Open it from the new icon on your home screen or desktop, not from a browser tab.
- **I want to remove it.** Remove it the way you remove any app on your device. That does not delete anything on your server.
- **Another person signed in on my device.** Press **Sign out** before you hand it over.
