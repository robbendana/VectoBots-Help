# Language

The **Language** setting chooses the language the app speaks to you in: its buttons, menus, labels and messages. You can have it in English or in Spanish (Mexico). It exists because many people work in two languages, and the app should be comfortable in the one you prefer.

## Before you start

- Nothing to buy. Free, and part of every plan.
- The choice is kept **on this device**, in this browser. If you open VectoBots on your phone and on your laptop, set it on each one.

## How to use it, step by step

**On a laptop:** click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**. **You** is selected. Scroll to the **General** section and find the **Language** row.

**On a phone:** tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then **You**, and find **Language** in the **General** section.

1. Open the **Language** list.
2. Pick one of the three choices.
3. The app changes at once. There is no Save button and no need to reload.

## The three choices

**Automatic.** The app follows your browser. If your browser prefers Spanish, the app is in Spanish. If it prefers English, or something the app does not have, the app is in English.

**English.** The app is always in English.

**Español (México).** The app is always in Mexican Spanish.

The names of the languages stay as written, so you can always find your own language even if the app is in the other one.

## What does not change

- **Your Bots answer in the language you write to them.** If you write in Spanish, they reply in Spanish, whatever the app language is. If you write in English, they reply in English. The app language only matters when a Bot cannot tell which language you wrote in: then it leans toward the app's language.
- **What you and your Bots have written stays as written.** Chat messages, Bot names, file names and the contents of your files are not translated. Only the app's own words change.
- **Voice typing has its own language.** It is a separate setting in the Notifications page. See [Voice typing]({{ '/voice-typing/' | relative_url }}).
- **The help pages.** The "Learn more" links open the help article in the language of the app, in English or Spanish.

## Real examples

- **A bilingual coach** keeps the app on **Automatic** and uses a Spanish browser on the laptop and an English one on the phone. Each device then looks right.
- **A therapist who sees clients in both languages** sets **English** or **Español (México)** and writes to Bots in whichever language the client note needs. The Bot follows.
- **A consultant who hands the laptop to an assistant** fixes the language, so it does not change with the browser.

Keep client details out of any language setting or Bot name. Language is only about the app's words.

## Costs and limits

- Free. No extra AI use.
- Two languages for now: English and Spanish (Mexico).
- Remembered per device. Clearing the browser's stored data can reset it.
- If you never chose, a new install shows English. An update never switches your language by itself.

## Common problems and fixes

- **Some words are still in English.** A small number of texts may not be translated yet. They stay in English rather than showing a wrong word.
- **The app is in English but my browser is Spanish.** The language may be set to **English**. Choose **Automatic**.
- **My Bot answers in the wrong language.** The Bot follows what you write. Write your message in the language you want back.
- **I changed it on my laptop but my phone is the same as before.** The setting is per device. Change it on the phone as well.
