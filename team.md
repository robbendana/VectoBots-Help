# Team members

## What it is and why it exists

**Team** lets a trusted person sign in to your VectoBots and talk to the Bots you choose to share with them. It is for an assistant, a business partner or a colleague who needs to work with your Bots, without sharing your own sign-in.

You stay the **only owner**. You read every conversation they have, and **every approval comes to you**. A team member can never approve anything.

## Read this first

- Team is **off by default**. Turn it on only for people you trust.
- **Team members' Bots can see your files.** Only turn this on for people you would trust with them.
- Turning Team off **signs every team member out at once**.
- Do not use Team for anyone who should not be near your files, and never share Bots that hold confidential client material with a person who does not need it.

## Before you start

- Team must be **part of your plan**. If not, the page says "Team is not part of your plan." The number of people you can add depends on your plan. The page shows "{n} of {max} used".
- You need an **app password** set first. The app says: "Set an app password first. Team members need one, or anyone could open the app."
- You need a **secure (HTTPS) address**, or a private network address. The app says: "Team needs a secure (HTTPS) address, or a private network address."
- A Bot can only be shared if it uses an **AI key**, not a plan sign-in, and its model has a price so a daily limit can be kept.

## Turn Team on

**On a laptop**

1. Click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**.
2. Click **Team** in the list on the left.
3. Turn on **Allow team members**.

**On a phone**

1. Tap **More** at the bottom, then **Settings**, then tap **Team**.
2. Turn on **Allow team members**. Use the back arrow to return to the list.

If something is missing, a line under the switch says what, for example the missing app password.

## Add a team member

1. With Team on, press **Add a team member**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name.** Letters, numbers and spaces, 1 to 40 characters.
   - **Login name.** 3 to 32 letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores. Must be unique.
   - **Job note.** A short label for you, like "accounts and client care". It gives no rights. Up to 60 characters.
   - **Sees what needs you.** Off by default. When on, the person sees what needs your attention on their own Bots. They still cannot answer.
   - **Shared Bots.** Tick the Bots they may talk to.
   - **Daily spend limit (USD).** Starts at 1. It is checked when a reply starts, so one long reply can end a little over.
3. Press **Make invite link**.
4. The app shows **Invite link for {name}**. Press **Copy link** and send it to them yourself. It works **once** and **expires in 48 hours**. It will not be shown again. If it is lost, press **New invite link** on their card.

## Manage each person

On each team member's card you can:

- See their status (**Invited**, **Active**, **Locked**), when they were last seen, and what they spent today.
- Edit the **Job note**, switch **Sees what needs you**, and change **Shared Bots**.
- Press **Clear lock** if they are locked out.
- Press **Read** to read their conversations with each shared Bot. Reading is view only.
- Press **New invite link** or **Remove**. Remove asks "Remove {name}? They are signed out at once. You keep their conversations." Confirm with **Remove team member**.

**Own Bots.** You can make a Bot just for one person: **New Bot for {name}**, or **Make an existing Bot theirs**. Only they use it, and they cannot change or delete it. A new Bot made this way has no apps switched on.

**AI keys.** To pay for their own Bots separately, add a key for that person under **AI keys**: choose the provider, paste the key, then **Save key**. It is stored on the server and never shown again. Without a key their own Bots do not run. Remove it with **Remove key**.

## The Team page

Once you have team members, **Team page** shows who is working, who needs you, and what each person spent today. **Open chat** takes you to the Bot's chat to answer an approval. **Read** shows the conversation.

## How approvals work

- A team member can never approve. Anything that needs approval comes to you, marked with their name ("Asked by {name}").
- While a team member talks to a Bot, your 30-minute and per-task approvals do **not** apply: every action asks you. Always-OK rules you wrote for that Bot still apply.

## Examples for a solo practice

- **An administrative assistant** who handles scheduling. Share one "Scheduling" Bot, set a small daily limit, and give a job note like "calendar and reminders". Leave **Sees what needs you** off at first.
- **A colleague who shares the practice.** Make an own Bot for them, add their own AI key, and keep your own client Bots unshared.
- **Confidentiality.** Do not share a Bot whose memory contains client information with someone who should not see it. Remember that you can read all their conversations, and tell them so.

## Costs and limits

- Team is part of some plans, and the number of members depends on your plan.
- Each member has a **daily spend limit** you set. When it is reached, their Bot stops with "The team member reached its daily limit."
- A person can have up to 20 Bots.
- Spend appears on the Team page ("Team spend today").

## Common problems and fixes

- **"Team is not part of your plan."** Check your plan.
- **"Turn Team on first."** Switch on **Allow team members**.
- **"You have reached the number of team members your plan allows."** Remove someone or change plan.
- **"That login is already used."** Pick another login name.
- **"A name is 1 to 40 letters, digits or spaces."** Shorten or simplify the name.
- **"The daily limit must be a number of dollars from 0 to 10000."** Fix the number.
- **"The provider refused that key. Check it and try again."** The key is wrong or expired.
- **"This Bot uses a plan sign-in. A shared Bot must use an AI key."** Give that Bot an AI key first.
- **Invite link lost.** Press **New invite link**.
